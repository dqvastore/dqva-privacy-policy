DQVA Store Analytics - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Purpose

DQVA Store Analytics is a private application designed to analyze sales, orders, inventory, expenses and profitability from an eBay seller account.

Information Used

The application may process order identifiers, product details, quantities, prices, inventory data and buyer identifiers needed for deletion compliance.

Storage and Security

Business information may be stored in Google Sheets. Cloudflare services are used for secure API integration and account deletion notification processing.

Data Sharing

Information is used for internal business analytics and is not sold to advertisers.

Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as necessary for legitimate business, accounting and legal purposes. Applicable deletion requests are handled in accordance with eBay requirements.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the DQVA Store Analytics owner through the associated eBay store.
